
Key Benefits & Pricing

• Elasticity: Scale resources up or down automatically as traffic changes.
• Pricing Models: Pay-as-you-go On-Demand billing, discounted Spot Instances for flexible workloads, and a Free Tier for getting started.
• Cost Management: Track and estimate spending using the AWS Pricing Calculator, AWS Budgets, and Cost Explorer.
